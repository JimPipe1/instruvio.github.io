About

A small studio that makes 3D animation and imagery for construction product manufacturers, contractors and specialist subcontractors.

We work almost entirely for the construction industry, and that focus is the point. A studio that mostly makes car adverts will give you something beautiful; it will not notice that the bracket is upside down.

What that means in practice

We read drawings. We know the difference between a specifier, an estimator and the person who will actually be holding the product at eight in the morning in the rain — and those three people need to be shown different things.

Every project starts from an accurate model, usually your own Revit or CAD data. That model is the single source for the animation, the catalogue stills and the technical details, which is why they never contradict each other, and why updating a product later is a small job rather than a new commission.

Who we work with

  • Product manufacturers who need to explain a system, not just show it
  • Specialist subcontractors bidding for work against cheaper-looking rivals
  • Main contractors producing method statements and site training material

Working with us

Most projects run to a fixed price agreed after we have seen your files. You approve a rough animatic before anything expensive is rendered, so there are no surprises at the end — and no invoice for changes you asked for at the storyboard stage.

We are happy to sign an NDA before you send anything.
