What we make

Every asset is built from one accurate 3D model, so your animation, renders and technical diagrams always agree with each other.

Installation animation

Step-by-step sequences that show a product being fitted the way it is actually fitted, at the pace it actually happens.

  • Full sequences for tender and trade stands
  • Short silent loops for product pages
  • Toolbox-talk cuts for site use

Product stills

Catalogue and campaign imagery of products that may not exist yet, shot through one consistent camera and lighting setup.

  • Whole ranges rendered to match
  • Transparent backgrounds for layouts
  • Print resolution as standard

Technical details and cutaways

Build-ups, sections and exploded views drawn to the specified dimensions, so a specifier measuring off the image gets the right answer.

  • Layered files you can re-label yourself
  • Consistent with your NBS specification
  • Updated from the model when the product changes

Modelling from CAD and BIM

We start from your Revit, IFC or CAD files where they exist, and from drawings and a sample where they do not.

  • Revit, IFC, STEP, SolidWorks and DWG accepted
  • One master model behind every deliverable
  • Tolerances and fixings kept accurate

Site and context visuals

Products and schemes placed in a real setting, with light matched to the orientation the image is meant to represent.

  • Hoarding and large-format artwork
  • Planning and tender imagery
  • Verified views on request

How a project runs

Four stages, fixed price, no surprises at the end.

  1. 01

    Technical review

    We work from your CAD, Revit or IFC files, or from drawings and a sample if the model does not exist yet.

  2. 02

    Modelling

    One accurate master model is built and checked against your specification and tolerances.

  3. 03

    Animatic

    You approve a rough sequence with timings and camera moves before we spend anything on rendering.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final animation, stills and cutaways, exported for web, trade stands and print.
