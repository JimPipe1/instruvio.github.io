**The brief.** A council was launching an online service and needed residents
to understand what it does, who can use it and how to get started. The video
had to meet accessibility requirements and be available in both official
languages.

## What we did

We wrote the script in plain English first, then worked with an Irish-language
translator and a native voiceover artist on the Irish version. On-screen text
was designed from the start to fit both languages. Colour contrast, pacing and
captions were checked against accessibility guidance.
