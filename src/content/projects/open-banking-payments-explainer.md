**The brief.** The sales team kept losing deals at the same point: merchants
did not understand how the payment method worked and assumed it was risky.

## What we did

We interviewed the sales and compliance teams to find the three questions
every merchant asked, and built the script around answering them in plain
language. The script went through compliance review before storyboarding, so
no approved wording changed late in the project.

Custom illustration in the client's brand makes the flow of money visible, and
a 30-second cut-down gives the sales team something to share on LinkedIn and
in follow-up emails.
