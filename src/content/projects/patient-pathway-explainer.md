**The brief.** Patients were arriving unprepared for day procedures, which
led to cancellations and anxious phone calls to the ward.

## What we did

Clinical and nursing staff reviewed every line of the script for accuracy.
We chose a warm, non-clinical illustration style and a slow, reassuring pace.
Key instructions, such as fasting times and what to bring, appear on screen as
well as in the narration.

The video was delivered with captions, audio description and a silent
waiting-room version.
